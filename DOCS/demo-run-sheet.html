Before you press record

Everything here comes from what the site actually shows; nothing in the script points at a thing that is not on screen.

What can go wrong, and the fallback

Speech queue stallsA single line can sit in GMI's queue for five minutes. The worker waits it out, so the recording keeps running; you cut the wait anyway.
Music 3.0 rate limitThe theme and the credits sometimes hit the requests-per-minute cap. The pipeline resubmits automatically; it just adds a minute.
Facts in the scriptThe research step reads Hacker News sources; a 1986 story has few of them, so some lines will be comedy rather than history. Say "it reads real sources" rather than "every fact is checked".
A failed run on cameraThe progress page shows a red Error box. Stop, fix, rerun: the create flow takes 30 seconds to redo and the edit hides the retake.
